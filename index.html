🔧 Troubleshooting & Help

We want your experience to be as smooth as possible. If you run into a small issue, here are some common things to check. Most problems are very easy to fix.

Problem: The Download Doesn't Start

Solution: Make sure you clicked the link correctly. Sometimes, a pop-up blocker on your internet browser might stop it. Check the top right corner of your browser for a pop-up blocked notification and allow it. Then try clicking the download link again.

Problem: Windows Shows a Blue Screen or a Warning

Solution: Because omaroll is a new application, Windows might show a message saying "Windows protected your PC". This is a common step with many new programs. If this happens, click on "More info", and then click the "Run anyway" button. This will let the program run normally.

Problem: My Videos Won't Play Sound

Solution: Make sure your computer's sound is not muted and your volume is turned up. Also, check that the video file itself is not damaged. If you're an advanced user, you can also check your computer's audio output settings.

Problem: The Program is a Little Big on My Screen

Solution: You can zoom out by pressing the "Ctrl" key and the minus sign "-" key at the same time. This will make all the icons and text smaller and neater.
